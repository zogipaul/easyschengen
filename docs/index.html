Frequently asked questions

How are travel days counted?
Both the entry day and exit day count as days spent in the Schengen Area.

Where is my information stored?
Trips and preferences stay locally on your device. No account is required.

Is this legal advice?
No. Easy Schengen is a planning aid. Always confirm current entry rules with the relevant official authorities before travelling.
