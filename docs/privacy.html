Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 July 2026

Easy Schengen respects your privacy. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Trip dates and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device. Trip information is shared only with the Easy Schengen Home Screen widget through Apple’s private App Group storage on the same device.

No accounts, analytics, or tracking

Easy Schengen does not use accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or external servers.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual trips in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data according to iOS behavior.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kaiserpaul@outlook.com.

Important notice

Easy Schengen is an informational planning aid and does not provide legal or immigration advice. Always confirm current rules with the relevant official authorities.
